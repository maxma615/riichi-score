This subset extends the original declaration subset with the glyphs used by
the settlement and final-ranking ceremony banners (点数结算, 流满贯, 满贯,
跳满, 倍满, 三倍满, 役满, 途中流局, 荒牌流局, 最终名次, 九种九牌 and
the digits 0-9). The original mahjong-brush.woff2 stays byte-identical so the
archived native asset manifest keeps validating.
